===== Stage C @ 2.400 GHz: Case 4 RFSPACE TSA600 Vivaldi (330 x 240 mm, 11 dBi @ 2.4 GHz datasheet) =====
TX frozen at the settled Stage A design: 52.2 mm dipole, 500 mm high, 20 dBm; receiver wired to its terminals
Jammer: 30 dBm, all inside the 20 MHz channel. J/S target >= 10 dB.
V2: perfect impedance match assumed (mismatch loss of the jammer and TX ports removed from S21); jammer port power 30 dBm.

TX alone (PEC)     : S11  -16.0 dB, Zin  68.8 +0.7j ohm, max realized gain  8.14 dBi
